Privacy Policy

Tracking with Lev (Android) · TEAM LEV · Effective November 26, 2026

Tracking with Lev is a baby care tracker that works without an account. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We (TEAM LEV) do not run a server for this app and we never receive your data.

What the app stores

The app keeps what you type in, only in its private storage on your phone:

  • Baby profiles: name, birth date and sex
  • Feeds, sleep and diaper logs, growth measurements, vaccines, milestones and notes
  • Care information you choose to add, such as your doctor's and an emergency contact's phone numbers, allergies and medicines
  • Your settings, such as sounds, reminders and text size

Other apps cannot read this storage. The app does not copy it to Google Drive or any cloud backup.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, photos or microphone.

Backups you make

When you tap Back Up Data, the app creates a backup file and saves it to a folder you pick, or hands it to an app you choose (for example Google Drive or email). Where that file goes is up to you, and the file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private. Restore From Backup reads a backup file you select.

The optional offline AI

Lev, the app's helper, can answer typed questions with a small AI model that runs on your phone. The first time you type a question, the app downloads the model file (about 533 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any download, Hugging Face's servers receive your phone's IP address and basic request details, as described in Hugging Face's privacy policy. The app sends no other information with that download.

After the download, questions and answers are processed entirely on your phone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. AI answers can be wrong and do not replace a doctor.

Reporting an AI answer

You can tap Report on any AI answer to hide it. If you also choose Email the Team, your phone's email app opens with that answer filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use reports only to improve the app's answers, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Notifications and permissions

  • Notifications: feed and vaccine reminders and the baby's-day status are scheduled on your phone.
  • Internet: used only to download the optional AI model, and to open health source links in your browser when you tap them.
  • Foreground service (media playback): lets Soothe's calming sounds keep playing with the screen off.
  • Run at startup: puts your reminders back after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: gentle feedback when you tap.

Deleting your data

Go to Family → Your Data → Delete All Data to erase everything the app has stored. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data, including the AI model. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected; delete those yourself.

Children

The app is made for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. It stores information about a baby only because a parent or caregiver enters it, and that information never leaves the phone.

Health information

Lev's guidance is general information based on NHS, AAP, CDC and WHO sources. It is not a diagnosis or a medical device. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
